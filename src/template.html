Claude Export Reader

Browse a Claude team data export: every person, conversation, project, artifact, design chat and memory. Everything runs inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded.

⇩
Drop your export files here
Select all the .zip files from the export (conversations, projects, memories, design_chats, frames, light_metadata) and drag them in. Unzipped folders work too.
Which files do I need?
  • conversations-000.zip: all chats (the big one).
  • light_metadata-000.zip: the people list (names and emails). Without it you see user IDs only.
  • projects-000.zip, memories-000.zip, design_chats-000.zip: projects with their docs, Claude's memory per person, Claude Design chats.
  • frames-000.zip, frames-001.zip, …: published artifacts with every saved version. They are large, but only the index is read until you open an artifact.
  • You can drop any subset. Missing parts just show as empty. Older export formats (projects.json, memories.json) also work.
  • Keep this tab open while you browse: the files are read on demand, so do not move or delete them meanwhile.
